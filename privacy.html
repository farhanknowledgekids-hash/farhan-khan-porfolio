Privacy notice

Last updated 16 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to the details you send through the enquiry form on this website. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who is responsible

Farhan Khan, an individual web designer based in Mumbai, decides how your details are used. To contact me about your data, use the enquiry form on the home page.

What is collected

  • Your name, your clinic's name and area
  • Your mobile number and, if you give it, your email address
  • The package you're interested in and any message you write

The form does not ask for, and you should not send, any patient or health information.

Why it is used

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, run your project. Your details are never sold, shared for marketing, or added to a mailing list.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are delivered by email through a form service that processes them on my behalf. They are kept for up to 12 months after our last conversation, or for as long as the law requires if you become a client, and are then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details I hold about you, correct them, or have them deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent stops further contact; it doesn't affect anything already done. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies and third parties

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or advertising scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash, so those services receive your device's IP address when the page loads, as with any website that uses them.

Demo concept pages

The forms on the Maison Ivoire, Little Banyan and Axial demo pages are for demonstration only. They check what you type in your browser and then discard it; nothing is sent or stored.
