How a project runs

  1. A 20-minute call

    At your clinic or by phone. We agree the package, the pages and what makes your clinic different. You get a written quote the same day.

  2. Your content checklist

    A short list: logo, doctor details, timings, treatments and photos. If you don't have photos, I'll suggest a photographer or licensed stock images.

  3. First draft

    A private preview link you can open on your phone. You and your team comment on it directly.

  4. Revisions

    Each round covers all your changes at once. Fixes to my own mistakes never count as a round.

  5. Launch and handover

    The site goes live on your domain. You receive the logins, the code, the PageSpeed report and a short guide to editing timings yourself.

Ownership, revisions and maintenance

The details clinics usually find out too late, written down before you pay anything.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your clinic's name, and hosting is billed to you directly. After the final payment you receive the full code, images and logins. There's no lock-in: you can move to another developer at any time.

What counts as a revision round?

One consolidated list of changes to text, images, colours or layout within the agreed pages. Extra rounds are ₹1,000 each. New pages beyond the package are ₹1,000 per page.

What happens after launch?

Every package includes free fixes for anything that isn't working as agreed, for 30, 60 or 90 days. After that, the optional care plan covers updates and small changes, or you can make changes yourself.

When is the delivery clock started?

Delivery times start once I have the deposit and your content checklist. If content arrives late, the date moves by the same number of days, and I'll tell you the new date in writing.

How are payments made?

50% when we start and 50% after you approve the final draft, before it goes live, by UPI or bank transfer. You get an invoice for both.

What won't you do?

I won't write fake reviews, promise a first-page Google ranking or guarantee a number of new patients. I also won't publish before-and-after photos without the patient's written consent.
