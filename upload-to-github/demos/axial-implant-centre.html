The implant process, from assessment to final tooth

Timings vary from patient to patient. Your dentist will give you your own timeline after the assessment.

  1. Day 1

    Assessment and 3D scan

    A full examination, a CBCT scan and a frank conversation about whether an implant is right for you.

  2. Within a week

    Digital plan and written quote

    The implant position is planned on your scan. You receive the plan, the options and an itemised cost.

  3. Surgery day

    Guided placement

    The implant is placed under local anaesthetic using a guide made from your plan. A temporary tooth is often possible.

  4. Typically 3 to 6 months

    Healing

    The implant bonds with the bone. Review visits check that healing is on track.

  5. Final visit

    Your permanent crown

    A crown is made from a digital scan, matched to your neighbouring teeth and fixed in place.

Which kind of implant treatment?

One missing tooth

A single implant and crown, without touching the healthy teeth on either side.

Several missing teeth

An implant-supported bridge: two implants can often replace three or four teeth.

A full arch

A fixed set of teeth on four to six implants, as an alternative to removable dentures.

What's used, and why it matters to you

Equipment only matters if it changes the result or the experience. Here's what each piece does.

Clinic equipment and what it does for patients
EquipmentWhat it means for you
CBCT 3D scannerShows bone height, width and nerve position, so the implant is planned on facts, not estimates.
Intraoral scannerDigital impressions instead of impression putty, for a more comfortable visit.
Printed surgical guideTransfers the digital plan to surgery, for accurate placement through a smaller opening.
Implant passportA record of your implant brand and size, so any dentist can maintain it in future.

What affects the cost of an implant

No two cases cost the same, so this page doesn't publish a price that might not apply to you. After your assessment you get an itemised, written quote, and the price doesn't change unless your treatment plan does.

  • Number of implants

    One tooth, a bridge or a full arch.

  • Bone grafting

    Needed only if the scan shows too little bone.

  • Implant system

    Different brands, all with documented track records.

  • Crown material

    Zirconia, porcelain-fused-to-metal or full ceramic.

  • Temporary teeth

    Whether you'd like a tooth during healing.

  • Extractions

    If a failing tooth must come out first.
