The approach

A beautiful smile is rarely the whitest or the straightest one. It's the one that looks as if it was always yours. Every plan here starts with photographs and a conversation about how you want to feel, before anyone mentions a shade.

We design your new smile digitally, show it to you on your own face, and make a trial version you can wear before anything is changed. Only once you're sure does the work begin.

“Nothing is changed until you have seen it, worn it and approved it.”

The studio's working principle

Treatments

  • Porcelain veneers Thin, hand-layered porcelain shells for shape, colour and symmetry. Usually two to three visits.
  • Composite bonding Chips, gaps and edges refined in a single visit, with little or no change to the natural tooth.
  • Professional whitening Supervised whitening with custom trays, planned so the result looks natural rather than flat.
  • Clear aligners Discreet straightening, often used to prepare teeth before veneers or bonding.
  • Digital smile design See your planned smile on your own photographs, then try it in the mouth before committing.

The studio

Two private suites, one patient at a time, and a lounge where nobody is called by a token number. Appointments run to time, and your consultation is never rushed into treatment on the same day.
